Entry for the make.fun competition **news-image-20261117**
("Sum up 17 November's world news in one image").

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the finished
image held in this repository to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a
1024×1024 JPEG (253,057 bytes, under the 1 MiB cap) stored under the
name `entry.png`, as the rules allow — the gate tells PNG from JPEG
by the file's first bytes. Two runs write byte-identical output.

## The image

A scroll-stopper news card: a red date band ("TUESDAY 17 NOVEMBER
2026"), one dominant lead visual filling the top half of the frame,
a massive two-line tabloid headline ("PUTIN'S BRIDGE / IS DOWN")
with a factual caption, and a bold 2x2 strip of the day's other top
stories, each with its own headline and one-line caption. All text
was typeset programmatically over generated editorial artwork, so
every word is crisp and legible at feed size.

## The stories (all reported on Tuesday 17 November 2026)

- **Lead — "PUTIN'S BRIDGE IS DOWN".** Zelenskyy hailed an
  "unprecedented operation" as a Kerch Bridge span fell into the sea;
  Russia struck Kyiv's bridges in reply. (BBC via Just Security
  Early Edition, 17 Nov 2026; Nhan Dan World News in Brief)
- **Iran drones target U.S. radar base.** Drones aimed at the Sheikh
  Isa air base in Bahrain; Bahrain says they were intercepted, with
  no casualties. (Reuters via Just Security / Nhan Dan)
- **Hack hits 178 top websites.** Malware injected via Optimizely
  reached the BBC, CNN, Zoom, HSBC and others, skimming card data
  and swapping crypto addresses. (Reuters, Decrypt, Indian Express)
- **Trump meets Quad leaders in Manila.** Japan, India and Australia
  attend; a Trump–Xi–Putin summit was set for Nov 19 in Shenzhen.
  (AP photo report, Outlook India; Indian Express)
- **U.N. debates ban on "killer robots".** 128 states in Geneva
  weigh a treaty to keep humans in control of AI weapons.
  (NYT / NPR via Just Security; Japan Times)
- **Also today (ticker):** Israel's Knesset replaces a Netanyahu
  ally as speaker 66–52 (Reuters); Mongolia's president says he
  will resign (AP); France's public-sector strike marches draw
  146,000 (Reuters).
